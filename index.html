Stop the TV asking “Allow?” for every new phone

On the TV: Settings › General › External Device Manager › Device Connection Manager › Access Notification › Off

Caution: after this, any phone or computer on the Wi-Fi can control the TV without asking.

Refused a phone by mistake? Same menu › Device List › set it to Allow, or delete it and connect again.

No remote to press Allow? Use the control button on the TV itself, or this page on a phone that is already connected.
